# VISUAL WORLD BIBLE — Master Style DNA

Canonical style DNA for all books. The single source of truth for style, color,
light, character, environment, and composition. All prompts (via `master-prompt.md`,
which is populated by `agents/5-illustration-director.md`) rely on this file.

---

## STYLE IDENTITY

Premium personalized keepsake children's picture book.
Simplified pastel watercolor nursery illustration style.
Feels like a luxury printed baby memory book by a professional children's publishing house.
Visual language inspired by classic watercolor picture books — NOT animation, comics,
cartoons or digital concept art. Every page: gentle, warm, emotional, timeless.
Evokes early childhood memories, family love, growth and discovery.

**Primary house style (Book 01 proven):** soft pastel watercolor with simple childlike forms,
large calm negative space, smooth cream paper washes, sparse foreground detail, and one clear
tiny focal character or emotional focal point. The image should feel sweet, airy, readable, and
nursery-safe — never hyper-detailed, realistic, busy, or technically showy.

---

## CORE ART MEDIUM

Traditional watercolor aesthetic, simplified for children's book production. Smooth pastel
washes, gentle pigment blooms, subtle paper texture, delicate pencil linework, soft colored-pencil
touches, natural edge variation, slight pigment pooling, and organic transitions.

Detail level is intentionally low-to-medium. Use clear silhouettes and readable simplified
shapes. Avoid tiny texture clutter: no botanical encyclopedia detail, no fur realism, no dense
grass/noise, no complex bark texture, no overworked backgrounds. This is important for upscaling
and for clean text overlay.

NOT: digital airbrush, vector, cel shading, hard graphic outlines, 3D rendering,
photorealism, AI-generated glossy effects.

---

## VISUAL MOOD

Warm · safe · cozy · tender · gentle · curious · wholesome · dreamlike but believable ·
emotionally comforting · quietly joyful.
Never dramatic. Never chaotic. Never overstimulating.

---

## COLOR SYSTEM

**Primary:** warm cream, ivory, soft beige, linen, warm white, oatmeal, light sand.
**Secondary:** sage green, dusty mint, muted eucalyptus, soft aqua, powder blue, misty teal.
**Accent:** blush pink, peach, apricot, soft coral, muted lavender, butter yellow.

**Behavior:** low saturation, pastel dominance, soft transitions, minimal contrast,
harmonious palette, gentle atmospheric blending. No neon, no harsh contrast.

---

## LIGHTING

Natural diffused daylight. Soft morning sunlight. Warm ambient illumination.
Subtle, painterly highlights. Nurturing and calm.
No dramatic shadows, no theatrical lighting, no strong contrast.

---

## CHARACTER DESIGN SYSTEM

`canonical.png` = single source of truth. Character consistency is mandatory; the child
remains visually identical throughout the entire book.

Required traits (default character — adapt per book to the actual reference):
dark brown curly hair with volume, large warm brown eyes, round cheeks, pink rosy
complexion, small button nose, gentle smile, soft baby proportions, chubby limbs,
rounded silhouette, friendly approachable look. Always ~1 year old.

Never: age up/down, alter facial proportions, redesign hairstyle, change eye color,
change overall silhouette.

### Facial expression language
Simple and readable: wonder, curiosity, joy, contentment, pride, excitement, gentle
surprise, sleepiness, affection. Subtle — avoid exaggerated cartoon emotions or facial
deformation.

---

## ENVIRONMENT DESIGN

Supports the child, never dominates. Typical: nursery, playroom, living room, garden,
park, bedroom, cozy indoor / safe outdoor. Clean, warm, welcoming.
No clutter, no visual noise, no complex architecture, no dark areas, no threatening elements.

For nature books, backgrounds should be simple and breathable: rounded hills, soft skies,
few plants, few props, smooth watercolor fields, and a single tiny focal creature when useful
(bee, frog, bird, rabbit, snail, butterfly, firefly). Let the empty space be beautiful.

### Props
Soft, child-friendly: wooden blocks, stuffed animals, blankets, picture books, small
chairs, wooden toys, building cubes, soft pillows, birthday decorations, toy animals.
Same pastel color system. Nothing competes visually with the child.

---

## COMPOSITION SYSTEM

Story-first. The child is always the primary focal point.
Eye moves: child → supporting elements → environment.
Balanced, breathable, generous negative space, obvious visual hierarchy. Avoid crowding.
Negative space should be illustrated and atmospheric, not empty. The best spreads feel like one
wide living panorama: the focal characters or objects sit on one side, while sky, water, field,
rain, stars, fireflies, plants, distant architecture, ground texture, or soft washes continue
quietly across the rest of the image. Keep the text side calmer and lower contrast, but still
visibly part of the same world.

For Tier S / no-child books, replace "child" with one tiny friendly focal creature or one simple
emotional object. Do not fill the page just because no child is present.

---

## PAGE LAYOUT SYSTEM

Default ratio: **2:1**. Single continuous illustration.
No split layouts, collage, frames, panels, borders, scrapbook composition, visible page
division. Every spread = one seamless painted scene.

---

## TEXT SAFE AREA SYSTEM

Most spreads: one side = storytelling area, the other = calm text-friendly area
(ALWAYS: story left, text-safe right — no alternation, so the Gemini watermark lands on the calm right side).
Environment naturally fades toward the text side.
Never a blank white area, never a separate background — the text zone stays part of the
same illustrated world: soft watercolor floor, light gradients, subtle atmospheric
details, gentle depth, minimal contrast. Readable yet fully illustrated.
Avoid the "half content / half empty" look. The text area should feel like a quiet continuation of
the panorama, similar to a soft sky, misty field, calm water, evening glow, or distant landscape
that can hold typography without looking unfinished.

---

## DEPTH SYSTEM

Foreground: few soft storytelling elements. Midground: main action. Background: light
watercolor shapes and atmosphere. Airy depth. Avoid strong perspective distortion and
cinematic angles.

---

## LINEWORK

Extremely delicate, thin pencil-like contours; many edges implied by paint rather than
outlines. No thick black outlines, no comic inking, no graphic contour emphasis.

---

## CAMERA

Eye-level child perspective, slightly lowered. Intimate storybook feeling.
Avoid dramatic perspectives, wide-angle distortion, cinematic action shots, extreme
closeups, top-down views. The viewer feels present in the child's world.

---

## QUALITY TARGET

Premium children's publishing quality through restraint, clarity, and softness. Luxury watercolor
keepsake book, but with simplified nursery illustration rather than realistic painting.
Hand-painted storybook appearance. Consistent character, palette and emotional tone.
Every illustration must look like it belongs to the same professionally illustrated book.

Best current reference set: the God's Love book's spreads (book 3 of the old pipeline). Use them as the house-style north star: pastel, simple, airy, soft, readable.
