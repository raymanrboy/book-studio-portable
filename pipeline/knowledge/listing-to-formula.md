# Listing Title → Formula Mapping

## How to Use

Read the listing title. Extract signals. Match to a formula.
If multiple formulas seem to fit, check the tiebreaker rules below.

---

## Signal Dictionary

| Signal word / phrase | Points to |
|---|---|
| First Birthday, 1st Birthday, Baby's First Year | Formula A |
| Milestone, Month by Month, Year One | Formula A |
| ABC, Alphabet, Alphabet Train, Letter Book | Formula F |
| Silly, Goose, Playful, Funny, Bouncy | Formula C2 |
| Adventure, Explorer, Curious, Discovery | Formula C1 |
| Brave, Courage, Little Hero | Formula C3 |
| God's Love, Nature, Ocean, Stars, Seasons | Formula B |
| Bedtime, Lullaby, Sleep, Dreamy | Formula B |
| Bible Stories, Storybook, Scripture, Tales | Formula D |
| Baptism, Christening, Naming Day, Godparent | Formula E |
| Daddy and Me, Mommy and Me, Family | Formula A (modified) |
| Blessing, Promise, Gift of Love, Covenant | Formula E |

---

## Tiebreaker Rules

**"Bible" + "Bedtime"** → Formula B (bedtime = meditative = Nature Metaphor tone)
**"Bible" + "Stories"** → Formula D (stories = anthology)
**"Bible" + "Baptism/Naming"** → Formula E (ceremony = Blessing Journey)

**"Birthday"** alone → Formula A
**"Birthday" + animal name** (bear, goose, bunny...) → Formula C
**"Birthday" + ABC/Alphabet** → Formula F (alphabet format wins over milestone format)

**"Daddy/Mommy and Me"** → Formula A, but replace milestones with shared moments
(e.g., "The day we went fishing", "The morning we made pancakes")

---

## Formula Quick Reference

| Formula | Name | Occasion | Arc type |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Milestone Journey | 1st Birthday | Chronological milestones |
| B | Nature Metaphor | Baptism, Bedtime, Love | One emotion × many images |
| C | Character Adventure | Birthday, Baby Shower | Light narrative, one day |
| D | Anthology | Religious gifting | Multiple standalone stories |
| E | Blessing Journey | Baptism, Naming Day | Series of promises to child |
| F | ABC Alphabet Square Book | First birthday, toddler learning, keepsake | 26 standalone letter moments + 2 dedication images |

---

## Examples

**"Personalized First Birthday Book, Custom Baby Name Story, 1st Birthday Keepsake"**
→ Signals: First Birthday, Baby Name, Keepsake → **Formula A**

**"Personalized Silly Goose Book for Baby, Custom Name, 1st Birthday"**
→ Signals: Silly, Goose, Baby → **Formula C2**

**"Personalized Bible Storybook with Name, Baptism Gift, Naming Day Gift"**
→ Signals: Bible Storybook (D) + Baptism/Naming Day (E) → **Formula E** wins (ceremony context overrides anthology)

**"Personalized Daddy and Me Adventure Book, Custom Father's Day Gift"**
→ Signals: Daddy and Me, Adventure → **Formula A modified** (shared moments, not solo milestones)
