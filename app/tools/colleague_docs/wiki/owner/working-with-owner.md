# Working with the owner

> The owner of this copy runs an Etsy shop with personalized children's books. He is not necessarily a programmer, and
> the program's interface is Ukrainian. He values a result treated "as if it were for oneself": tidy, well thought out,
> nothing extra. (The first owner's notes were written down in his own copy; this page is the neutral version.)

## Communication

- Reply in the user's language, in plain words, with no jargon or function names in explanations. A report should say
  what changed for him, what was checked, what wasn't, and what he needs to do (e.g. restart the program).
- Be honest about what couldn't be checked (real smoothness in a visible window, a real drag-and-drop from Explorer,
  live orders).
- Don't ask unnecessary questions: if the answer is obvious, just do it and say so briefly.

## Working modes

- **"Just analyze, don't change anything" / "discussion mode"** — analysis and conversation only, no file changes.
- **Start only on command.**
- Big ideas are talked through as a dialogue before anything is built.
- Plans, agreements, and specs go **into the project's files** (this wiki), not just into an agent's memory.

## The owner's computer

Everything the program needs lies next to it in `runtime\` (see [the portable copy](../dev/portable.md)). Before doing
something that could touch his shop or accounts, ask: the program talks to Etsy (read-only), Printify and Gelato with
his keys.
