# Step 0 — Formula and Tier

Read the listing / brief and pick the storytelling formula and the tier. Nothing else.

Use [listing-to-formula.md](../../../../pipeline/knowledge/listing-to-formula.md) (signal words and tiebreakers) and the
formula files in [formulas/](../../../../pipeline/knowledge/formulas/). If the reference product is clearly an anthology of
standalone stories, choose D even when a ceremony word is present — match the product, not the word.

**Tier**
- **S** — no recurring human child: the default for every new book (animal books, the owner's decision of 2026-11-01),
  and for anthologies where the figures change per spread. Skips step 1.
- **H** — one human child is the recurring hero (older books). Runs step 1.
- **Formula F (ABC)** — Tier S by default and its own route: [abc.md](abc.md).

## `agent/formula.md`

The Workshop reads `Tier:` from it (and ticks its first step), step 2 copies it into the blueprint header:

```
Formula: [A / B / C / D / E / F] — [name]
Occasion: [1st birthday / baptism / Christmas / ...]
Tone: [playful / meditative / ceremonial / nostalgic]
Tier: [H | S]
Theme / central idea: [one sentence]
```
