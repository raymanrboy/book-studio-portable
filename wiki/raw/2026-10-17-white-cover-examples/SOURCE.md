## The formula they share

1. **Ground:** clean warm white or ivory paper over most of the picture — never a full dark or colored scene.
2. **Title space:** the upper 40–55% stays plain white (only a few tiny stars, sparkles, confetti or a bird at its edges).
3. **The picture sits low:** the characters and a small piece of their world sit in the lower part, large, close and emotional (a hug,
   a smile, a prayer) — readable at thumbnail size.
4. **Two ways to place it:** (a) a **vignette** — a small scene whose edges dissolve softly into the white, no frame (1, 3); or (b)
   **characters alone on white** with a soft ground shadow and a few scattered props (4, 5).
5. **One tall framing element** at a side edge is common (the tree in 1, the star burst in 3, the dove in 5).
6. **Color is concentrated** on the characters; the rest is pale and airy, high-key, bright.
