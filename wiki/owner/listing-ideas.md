welcome-to-the-world, ring bearer (the pair of his flower girl), "Dream Big", the birth-month book. What only LunaBooksUS has: star signs,
search & find, ABC, big feelings, pets, graduation, Night Before Christmas. What only TheChildhoodKeeper has: twins, fairy, cowboy, the
woodland autumn/birthday books with a calendar, **guest books and greeting cards**. What the owner has and they don't: the whole Christian
prayer/Bible line, vehicle bedtime (trucks, ships, trains, airplane), safari, ocean, farm, godparent, guardian angel, nativity.

## Rules of the game (from both shops)

- **One book → many listings.** The same interior gets a different cover/title/tags per buyer or occasion (LunaBooksUS: baptism ×12 titles,
  Christmas ×10, one per family role). That is how two a day is reachable — see the "Listings" column.
- **The buyer picks a look for the child** from a numbered chart (16 faces at TheChildhoodKeeper, 5–7 per gender at LunaBooksUS), writes a
  dedication, and says who it is from; some books take a birth date (the calendar page) or two names (twins).
- TheChildhoodKeeper's 10-picture listing: cover on a shelf with a plush toy · cover on a blanket with 4 claims (made just for you, premium pages…) ·
  "30 pages, name on every page, every page in rhyme" with two spreads · "vibrant, fade-resistant colours, matte hardcover" · the cover in a crib
  · hands holding the book · the dedication page with arrows ("choose who it's from") · **three sizes** (11″ hardcover, 8″ hardcover or softcover,
  5.5″ softcover) · "easy to personalize" in 3–4 steps · a refund promise. Compare with the owner's [six slides](../features/slides.md).
