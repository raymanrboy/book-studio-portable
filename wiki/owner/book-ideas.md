# Book ideas

> The owner's shelf of ideas for new books: links to books and listings he finds, and the key ideas he likes in each.
> Nothing here is a plan or a task — an idea only becomes work when the owner says "start".

## How to add an entry

The owner sends a link (or a screenshot) and says in words what he likes. The agent adds an entry **at the top of
"Ideas"** below, in the owner's words (Ukrainian is fine), without rewriting his meaning:

```markdown
### Short name of the idea — YYYY-MM-DD
- **Source:** link (or "screenshot", `raw/…` file if one was saved)
- **What it is:** one line — the book / listing / niche
- **What I like:** the key ideas, as the owner said them
- **For our books:** how it could become ours (only if the owner said so)
- **Status:** idea · in discussion · taken into work (→ Workshop book) · dropped
```

- Add the entry; don't analyse or expand it unless asked ("analyze" mode — [working with the owner](working-with-owner.md)).
- A screenshot or a long text goes to `wiki/raw/` first ([ingest rules](../dev/wiki-rules.md)).
- When an idea becomes a Workshop book, set its status and link the book; don't delete the entry.
- Several ideas that share a trick (a layout, a hook, a series format) — add a line to "Patterns" so it isn't lost.

## Patterns

- **One shared visual style, one author** (2026-10-11): the first four ideas (bears and grandma, foxes and daddy, big sister, welcome to the
  world) are all from one Etsy shop, **TheChildhoodKeeper** (found out 2026-10-11 from the listing pages), and are liked first of all for the same drawing style — soft, warm, illustrated; to be analysed and named once, then reused. The owner's guess: it is made with
  Nano Banana, and it looks like pencil plus something else, not plain paints.
- **A personalized rhyme with the name in it, a character on every page, a matte hardcover** — the listings' shared format.

### Grandma & grandchild bears — visual style — 2026-10-11
- **Source:** Etsy listing <https://www.etsy.com/listing/4577876955> ("Personalized Grandma Storybook for Grandchild, Custom Name Book,
  Nana Keepsake, Grandmother Birthday Gift from Grandchild"); five listing screenshots in
  [raw/2026-10-11-grandma-bear-listing/](../raw/2026-10-11-grandma-bear-listing/1.webp) (1–5.webp).
- **What it is:** a personalized book about a grandma bear and a little bear (the cover reads "Grandma & David — Every Day Is Magic"); the
  name is inserted into the rhymed text ({NAME}); matte hardcover; each spread is a picture on one side and a short rhyme on the other.
- **What I like:** the **visual** very strongly — the drawing style itself. Wants to copy the style (not the story): the picture, the
  look, and so on. It was surely made with Nano Banana. The open question is what to *call* this style: "paints" is what we call it now,
  but it isn't really paints — more a drawing format; the owner's guess is pencil plus something else.
- **For our books:** to analyse the style, name it exactly, and reuse it in our prompts ("analyze and use, work out the guess").
- **Status:** made (2026-10-16). The style is analysed and saved as the preset **WP — Watercolor & Pencil Cottage** (`pipeline/playbook/knowledge/media-presets.md`): transparent watercolor over soft warm-brown pencil lines, colored-pencil strokes for fur and knit, muted cottage-garden colors, the text side a plain watercolor wash. The book is in the Workshop as `46.grandma-and-me` ("Grandma & {Name}: Our Happy Days", Tier S, our own scenes and rhymes).
