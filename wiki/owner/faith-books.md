## Rules these books keep

- **52:** Mary, Joseph and the Baby are never drawn: the stable shows only golden light from its doorway; no angels, no crosses.
- **53:** Noah is in the text only, never drawn: tools rest on planks, the dove carries the story. The storm stays calm and safe.
- **54:** no hawk or fox; every scare ends under the wing on the next spread.
- **55:** the cub has no mane, so one book for boys and girls; no hunting and no fangs.
- The wording is ecumenical; the Bible references are in each book's `text-critic-report.md`.
