# Ideas backlog

> The owner's shelf of ideas for new books and listings, open ones only. The owner posts two listings a day, so he needs a wide, ever-growing stock; one book can give several listings. An idea becomes work only when he says "start". Books already in the Workshop are on [Workshop books](workshop-books.md), not here.

## How to add an entry
The owner sends a link or a screenshot and says in words what he likes. The agent adds a row (or, for a longer find, an entry at the top of "Notes" below) in his words, without rewriting his meaning and without analysing unless asked:

```markdown
### Short name — YYYY-MM-DD
- **Source:** shop or listing, in words
- **What it is:** one line
- **What I like:** the key ideas, as the owner said them
- **For our books:** how it could become ours (only if the owner said so)
- **Status:** idea · in discussion · taken into work (→ Workshop book) · dropped
```
When an idea becomes a Workshop book, move it to [Workshop books](workshop-books.md). A trick shared by several ideas goes into "Patterns".

## Patterns
- **One shared visual style, one author:** the owner liked the cozy, soft, warm illustrated style of the TheChildhoodKeeper listings first of all; it is written as presets in `pipeline/knowledge/media-presets.md`.
- **The shared format:** a personalized rhyme with the name in it, a character on every page, a matte hardcover.
- **One book, many listings:** the same interior gets a different cover, title and tags per occasion or buyer (LunaBooksUS: one interior for Uncle, Aunt, Mommy; baptism under many titles). Two listings a day are reachable only this way; the real art work is far smaller than the number of listings.
- **The buyer picks a look** for the child from a numbered chart of faces (TheChildhoodKeeper: 16; LunaBooksUS: 5–7 per gender), writes a dedication and says who it is from; some books take a birth date or two names.

## Seasonal order
Posted dates matter more than the rest. Christmas first; then Valentine's, Easter, Mother's Day in May, graduation, Father's Day in June, back to school, Halloween, Thanksgiving. The rest has no date.

## Turned down
- Program features: upscaling for orders, library pages instead of Canva, a demo book without Canva, a character checker, a print-quality journal, profit by book, deadlines, notifications, sales analytics, a morning summary, the program rendering book pages instead of Canva (the owner keeps control).
- Not to copy: a Disney character (a Winnie the Pooh book in LunaBooksUS's shop), or a shop's own characters, scenes and text.
